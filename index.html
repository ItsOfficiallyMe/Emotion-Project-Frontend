About SENTIO

SENTIO bridges the gap between human emotion and artificial intelligence. This innovative project leverages state-of-the-art Computer Vision and Deep Learning technologies to achieve highly accurate Emotion Recognition.

By mapping complex facial expressions to emotional states in real-time, SENTIO pioneers a new era of Personalized Music Recommendation. It doesn't just play songs; it curates an auditory experience tailored strictly to how you feel in the present moment.
